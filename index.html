    Reading it. Darker cell = more incidents. Colour carries one thing only (the count); the three classes are separated by space and labels, not by hue. In the time bars, gray is the whole row and the dark overlay is the part you are pointing at.

    Caveats. “Vendor family” is grouped by hand from the dataset's Affected agentic AI column, which also contains people and organisations harmed by AI output; those fall under “Other / non-vendor”. Counts are logged incidents, not rates.
